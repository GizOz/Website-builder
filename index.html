The house
under the arches

Keshet means arch. Ours stands on the square where Herzliya meets the sea, with a view of the water from every table.

Mornings begin with carefully pulled espresso and ceremony teas. By afternoon the tables fill with long brunches and fresh, generous plates.

At sunset the house turns to the bar: a Spritz de Shalit, a chilled rosé from Rioja, a bottle of Crémant shared over the last of the light.
