On De Shalit Square · Herzliya

Sea view.
Good coffee.
Easy days.

Cafe Keshet sits on Hasharon Beach, steps from the water. Come for the morning coffee our regulars rave about, stay for fresh, generous plates, and linger into the evening over a spritz, a glass of rosé or something from the bar.

Romantic dinners, lazy family brunches, events by the sea — or just grab and go on your way to the beach.
