Sand at your feet, espresso in hand.

Keshet sits on De Shalit Square, right on Hasharon Beach. Mornings start with the coffee our regulars come back for. Afternoons are long brunches and burgers. When the sun drops into the sea, it turns into a bar: spritzes, frozen margaritas and rosé on ice.

Come for a romantic dinner or a family brunch, catch one of our events, or grab a cold brew on your way to the water.
