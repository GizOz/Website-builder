# Website-builder — rules for Claude

## Design rule: no "standard Claude" design

Every site built here must have its own visual identity, drawn from the business itself
(its logo, location, materials, name, photos). Do not fall back on the look that AI-built
pages share. Before designing, browse current design trends and real sites in the client's
category for ideas.

Avoid these default patterns unless the client's own brand explicitly calls for them:

- Cream / beige / off-white page backgrounds with terracotta or rust accents
- Indigo / violet gradients, soft radial "glow" blobs
- Pill-shaped (fully rounded) buttons, badges and tag chips
- Small uppercase, letter-spaced "eyebrow" labels above every heading
- Centred hero with a status badge, big title and two side-by-side buttons
- Grids of soft rounded cards with thin borders (feature cards, review cards)
- Three-column "feature" card rows
- Emoji used as icons
- Wave SVG section dividers
- Fade-up-on-scroll reveal animations on every block
- Decorative circles behind photos
- Italic serif display headlines as the "elegant" default
- One safe sans-serif for everything

Instead: derive layout, shapes and motion from a concept specific to the business, use
sharp or purposeful shapes, show content directly (no hidden tabs when a list fits), and
make one primary action dominant.

## Colour rule

Use the client's own brand palette (sample it from their logo, menu, signage or photos)
and keep it exact. Do not "upgrade" a brand to luxury with generic metallic or champagne
gold, near-black, or other stock premium palettes. Luxury comes from space, type and
restraint, not from swapping the brand's colours.
